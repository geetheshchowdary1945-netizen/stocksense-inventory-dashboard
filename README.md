# StockSense - Inventory Dashboard

## Project Overview
StockSense is a smart inventory management dashboard for small businesses. It helps manage products, track sales, monitor suppliers, and make better purchasing decisions.

## Problem Statement
**E6 - Small Business Inventory & Supply Dashboard**

Manual inventory tracking can cause stockouts, overstocking, poor supplier visibility, and incorrect reorder decisions.

## Proposed Solution
StockSense combines inventory tracking, sales management, supplier orders, and stock alerts in one dashboard. It helps business owners identify shortages and plan replenishment.

## Key Features
- Inventory management: Add, edit, search, and track products.
- Sales tracking: Record sales and update stock.
- Low-stock alerts: Identify products requiring replenishment.
- Smart reordering: Suggest quantities based on sales and supplier lead times.
- Supplier management: Track suppliers, purchase orders, and deliveries.
- Reports: Visualize inventory and sales information.

## Technology Stack
- React and TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Vite
- Recharts
- Firebase integration

## Target Users
Small shops, cafes, pharmacies, and online sellers.

## Benefits
- Reduce stockouts and excess inventory.
- Improve supplier visibility.
- Simplify inventory management.
- Support better purchasing decisions.

## Future Scope
Barcode scanning, mobile apps, POS integration, automatic purchase orders, demand forecasting, and multi-store management.
