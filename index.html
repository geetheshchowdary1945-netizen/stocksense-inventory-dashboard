Smart Replenishment & Reorder Formulas

StockSense eliminates stockouts and overstock by dynamically computing daily demand velocities and supplier lead times.

Automated Math Engine
1. Average Daily Sales (ADS)
ADS = Units Sold Last 30 Days / 30
Measures how quickly this specific item moves off your shelves.
2. Reorder Point (ROP)
ROP = (ADS × Supplier Lead Time) + 20% Safety Buffer
When current inventory drops below this threshold, an order must be placed immediately.
3. Suggested Order Quantity
Qty = [ADS × (Lead Time + 14 Days Cover)] - Current Stock
Replenishes enough inventory to sustain lead time plus a two-week operational buffer.
4. Slow-Mover / Overstock Risk
Stock > (ADS × 60 Days) OR 0 Sales with >20 Stock
Highlights items that tie up cash and may require clearance discounting.
